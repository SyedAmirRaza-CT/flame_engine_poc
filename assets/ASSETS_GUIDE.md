# Assets Guide for Bird Life Simulation

To replace the placeholder shapes with real graphics, add the following files to the corresponding directories:

## Sprites (assets/sprites/)
- `bird_idle.png`: 4-8 frames of idle animation.
- `bird_walk.png`: 4-6 frames of walking animation.
- `bird_fly.png`: 4 frames of flying animation.
- `bird_eat.png`: 4 frames of eating animation.
- `bird_bath.png`: 4-6 frames of bathing animation.

Recommended size: 32x32 or 64x64 pixels per frame.

## Environment (assets/images/environment/)
- `grass_tile.png`: A tileable grass texture for the background.
- `tree_1.png`, `tree_2.png`: Cartoon style trees.
- `flower_red.png`, `flower_yellow.png`: Small flower sprites.
- `rock_1.png`: Rock sprites for decoration.

## Pond (assets/images/pond/)
- `pond_base.png`: The main water body.
- `pond_ripples.png`: Optional sprite sheet for water animation.

## UI (assets/images/ui/)
- `button_feed.png`: Custom icon for the feed button.
- `button_bath.png`: Custom icon for the bath button.
- `stat_icons.png`: Icons for happiness, hunger, energy, etc.

## Audio (assets/audio/)
- `ambient_park.mp3`: Peaceful birds and wind background sound.
- `bird_chirp.wav`: Short sound for happiness reactions.
- `water_splash.wav`: Sound for entering the pond.
- `eating.wav`: Sound for feeding.
